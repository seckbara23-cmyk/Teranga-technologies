## 8. Recommendations (implemented in 05B unless marked owner)

1. Redirect the `.vercel.app` production host and trailing-slash URLs to the canonical URLs (`vercel.json`).
2. Owner: make `www` a 308 permanent redirect in Vercel → Domains.
3. Keyword-led titles and descriptions, EN and FR (`docs/TERANGA-05B-search-strategy.md`).
4. Service names inside the Services headings; Service structured data.
5. One truthful structured-data graph on every indexable page (Organization, WebSite, WebPage); remove the LocalBusiness typing and unsupported properties.
6. Address the four buyer segments explicitly; replace the generic lines.
7. Correct the "In production" labels; state Radiora's verified status.
8. Add `favicon.ico`.
9. Owner: Search Console (DNS verification), Bing Webmaster Tools, sitemap submission, LinkedIn page; the email and ScolaTech decisions.

## 9. Blockers

None for the technical work. For credibility, the **EduSen/ScolaTech naming** and the **missing relationship and authorisation for each portfolio product** cannot be resolved without the owner. The site keeps today's factual wording until then.
