About

An engineering partner from Dakar, working in French and English

Teranga Technologies designs and builds software for startups, growing SMEs and enterprise teams in Senegal, across Africa and internationally.

Our name

Why “Teranga”

“Teranga” is the Wolof word for hospitality, a value at the heart of Senegalese culture.

For us it means treating your product with care, candour and follow-through: clear estimates, honest timelines and software that keeps working after we hand it over.

What we stand for

Principles behind every engagement

  • 01

    Engineering focus

    Architecture, testing and security are part of every estimate, not optional extras.

  • 02

    Maintainability

    Version-controlled code, documented architecture and clean handovers, so you are never locked in.

  • 03

    Responsible AI

    AI where it pays off, tested on real cases, with people in charge of decisions that matter.

  • 04

    Bilingual delivery

    Discovery, specifications, delivery and support in French or English.

Working together

How we work with clients

  • You own what we build

    Ownership is set out in the contract. Our standard approach: you own the custom code and deliverables we build for you once the project is paid.

  • You always know where things stand

    Regular demos and honest timelines; we tell you early if anything puts the plan at risk.

  • We fit your situation

    We can work alongside your team, extend an existing codebase, or hand everything over with documentation and a walkthrough.
