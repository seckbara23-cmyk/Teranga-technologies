A grounded example

Radiora Medical: from dictation to a signed report

In Radiora Medical, a product we built, radiologists dictate from a computer, a phone or an audio file. The AI structures the dictation into a draft with Indication, Technique, Results and Conclusion.

  • The AI never diagnoses and never signs.
  • The radiologist reviews, edits, validates and signs every report.
  • Data is isolated per establishment, with a complete audit trail.

How we keep it reliable

Safeguards designed in from the start

  • In production: Radiora Medical

    Human-in-the-loop approval

    High-stakes outputs stay drafts until a qualified person reviews, edits and approves them.

  • How we work

    Evaluation and quality assurance

    Test sets built from real cases; accuracy, tone and failure modes checked before launch and monitored after it.

  • In production: Radiora Medical and EduSen

    Privacy, access control and auditability

    Access control, isolation between organisations and audit trails, so every record and action can be traced.

  • How we work

    Operating-cost management

    Right-sized models per task, caching and usage limits, so the cost per request is known before you scale.

Where to start

One workflow, one number, then scale

  1. 1

    Pick a workflow and a metric

    One process and the number it should move: time per task, error rate or response time.

  2. 2

    Prove it on real cases

    A prototype tested against a set of real, anonymised examples before anyone relies on it.

  3. 3

    Roll out with oversight

    Human approval where the stakes are high, monitoring, and a cost per request you can plan for.
