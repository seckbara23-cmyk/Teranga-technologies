How we work

From first call to a product in use

Four steps, with a clear decision point before any build starts.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We clarify the outcome you want, your users, your constraints and what success looks like.

    Your part
    A 30-minute call with the people who know the process.
    You receive
    A shared problem statement.
  2. 2

    Define

    We turn it into a written scope with milestones, a delivery plan and a cost estimate.

    Your part
    Review the scope and decide whether to proceed.
    You receive
    Scope, plan and estimate.
  3. 3

    Build and validate

    Short iterations, with working software demonstrated regularly and tested before each release.

    Your part
    Feedback at each demo; priorities adjusted as you learn.
    You receive
    Working, tested increments.
  4. 4

    Launch and improve

    Deployment, monitoring and handover, then maintenance and improvements if you want them.

    Your part
    The go-live decision.
    You receive
    A production release and its documentation.
