Healthcare AI · Senegal and West Africa

Radiora Medical

AI-assisted radiology reporting for clinics and imaging centres.

Visit the product (opens in a new tab) Discuss a similar project

Context

Imaging centres produce reports that must follow a consistent structure and be validated by a radiologist.

The problem

Turning free dictation into a clean, structured report takes typing and formatting time that radiologists would rather spend reading images.

What we built

  • Dictation from a computer, a phone (scan a QR code, no app to install) or an audio file
  • AI structures free dictation into Indication, Technique, Results and Conclusion as you speak
  • Spoken corrections and free editing, then export to PDF or Word
  • Report templates, a report list and a review-and-sign step

Engineering decisions

  • Data isolated per establishment: each clinic sees only its own records
  • A complete audit trail of actions on each report
  • Reports stay drafts until signature, so unvalidated text is never final

Human oversight

The AI never diagnoses and never signs. The radiologist reviews, edits, validates and signs every report.

Results

Quantitative outcomes are not yet published. We share current figures on a discovery call.

Education SaaS · Senegal

EduSen

School management SaaS designed for private schools in Senegal.

Visit the product (opens in a new tab) Discuss a similar project

Context

Private schools manage enrolment, attendance, tuition and report cards, often across paper registers and spreadsheets.

The problem

Re-entering the same information in several places makes records hard to keep current and term report cards slow to produce.

What we built

  • Student records with admission numbers, searchable in seconds
  • Daily digital attendance by class, with notifications to families
  • Tuition tracking with digital receipts
  • Grade entry, automatic averages and term report cards

Data and administration

  • Strict data isolation between schools
  • Logged access to student records
  • A real-time administration dashboard
  • A French interface aligned with Senegalese school conventions

Who it is for

Built for school administrators and teachers, with families kept informed of absences and results.

Results

The platform is in early adoption; quantitative outcomes are not yet published.
