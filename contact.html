FAQ

Questions clients ask before starting

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, integrations and the level of polish you need at launch. After the discovery call, we send a written estimate tied to clear milestones. For larger products we often recommend a phased plan, so you invest in the first release before committing to the full roadmap.

How long does an MVP take?

A focused first version is typically scoped for 4–8 weeks. We confirm the timeline once the scope is agreed, and we tell you early if anything puts it at risk.

Do you work with clients outside Senegal?

Yes. We work remotely with clients in Senegal and internationally, in French or English. Dakar is on UTC+0 all year, which makes collaboration with Europe straightforward and leaves a good daily overlap with North America.

How do you use AI responsibly?

We start from a specific workflow, test output quality on real cases before launch, keep people in charge of high-stakes decisions and control who can access what data. In Radiora Medical, for example, the AI structures reports but only a radiologist can validate and sign them.

Can you work on our existing product or with our team?

Yes. We can extend an existing codebase, integrate with your current systems, or add AI features to a product already in use. We start by reviewing what exists so recommendations are grounded in your actual code and constraints.

Who owns the code?

Ownership is set out in the contract. Our standard approach is that you own the custom code and deliverables we build for you once the project is paid, with documentation so any team can take it over.

What happens after launch?

We can handle maintenance, monitoring, updates and new features on an ongoing basis, or hand everything over to your team with documentation and a walkthrough.
