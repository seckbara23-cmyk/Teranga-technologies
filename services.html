Services

Software services built around business outcomes

Six ways we work with startups, growing SMEs and enterprise teams. Each starts from the problem, not the technology.

  • SaaS development
  • AI integration and automation
  • Custom web applications
  • Mobile applications
  • Website design and development
  • Maintenance and ongoing support
01

SaaS development

Launch a SaaS product you can sell, scale and maintain.

The problem

A product idea or an internal tool needs to become software customers pay for, without a rewrite at the first sign of growth.

What we deliver

  • Multi-tenant architecture with secure accounts and roles
  • Subscriptions, billing integration and admin tools
  • A focused first version, then a roadmap

Typical uses

  • An MVP for a funded startup
  • Turning an internal tool into a product
  • Multi-site platforms for clinics, schools or agencies

The outcome

A product in customers’ hands, on foundations that support the next releases. A focused MVP is typically scoped for 4–8 weeks once the scope is agreed.

02

AI integration and automation

Put AI to work inside your real workflows.

The problem

Teams lose hours to repetitive reading, typing and sorting, and generic AI tools do not fit their data, language or approval rules.

What we deliver

  • Models connected to your documents, data and tools
  • Structured extraction and drafting with review steps
  • Quality testing, cost controls and audit trails

Typical uses

  • Turning dictation and documents into structured records
  • Drafting replies and reports for your team to review
  • Searching internal knowledge in French and English

The outcome

Time saved on a specific, measured process, with people keeping the final say.

03

Custom web applications

Replace spreadsheets and manual steps with one reliable system.

The problem

Operations run on spreadsheets, email threads and re-typing, so nobody has a reliable, current picture.

What we deliver

  • Portals, dashboards and back-office tools
  • Integrations with the systems you already use
  • Payments and messaging such as Wave, Orange Money or WhatsApp

Typical uses

  • Operations dashboards and reporting
  • Client, partner and staff portals
  • Approval and booking workflows

The outcome

One source of truth, fewer manual steps and real-time visibility for your team.

04

Mobile applications

Put your service in your customers’ pocket.

The problem

Customers and field teams need to act from their phones, but the platform was not designed for it.

What we deliver

  • iOS and Android apps for customers or field teams
  • Sign-up, payments and notifications
  • The same backend as your web platform

Typical uses

  • Customer apps for bookings, orders and payments
  • Field apps for data collection and reporting
  • A companion app for an existing SaaS

The outcome

A mobile experience that shares data and rules with the rest of your product.

05

Website design and development

A website that earns trust and turns visits into conversations.

The problem

The website undersells the company: slow, hard to update, unclear about what you do, or in only one of your customers’ languages.

What we deliver

  • Clear positioning and page structure
  • Fast, accessible, bilingual pages
  • A working contact path with lead notifications

Typical uses

  • Company and product websites
  • Landing pages for launches and campaigns
  • Bilingual French/English sites

The outcome

A credible site that prospects understand quickly and can contact easily.

06

Maintenance and ongoing support

Keep what you launched secure, current and improving.

The problem

After launch, dependencies age, small issues pile up and nobody owns the improvements.

What we deliver

  • Monitoring, updates and security patches
  • Fixes and small improvements on an agreed cadence
  • Documentation kept up to date

Typical uses

  • Products we built
  • Existing products we take over after a code review

The outcome

A product that stays reliable while it evolves with your business.
